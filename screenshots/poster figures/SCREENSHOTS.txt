Poster Figures -- SEED symposium poster
=========================================

Figures built specifically for the poster (screenshots/pygame/ holds
the general project screenshots this folder is split out from).


pillar1_figure.png -- FTC field + 5-algorithm comparison (poster Pillar 1)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two-panel figure built by generate_pillar1_figure.py, not captured from
an interactive session like screenshots/pygame's scenarios. Left panel:
an actual FTC-scale (144in x 144in, 6in cells = 24x24) grid from
ftc.field.build_grid("cluttered"), planned with nav.algorithms.astar,
robot drawn mid-route with its real rotated footprint (the same corner
math ftc/field.py's collision check uses) over the *eroded* (real,
uninflated) obstacle cells. Right panel: Dijkstra/A*/RRT/RRT* planning
time and quality stats, recomputed live from benchmark_results/
results.csv (nav/benchmark.py's 20-trial run) rather than hardcoded.
ftc_field_pillar1_inprogress.png is the same left panel alone, saved as
a smaller standalone crop.

Regenerate after any change to ftc/field.py, ftc/match.py,
pygame_app/ftc_viz/field_view.py (anything touching obstacle inflation,
erosion, or footprint/collision geometry), or after re-running
nav/benchmark.py:

    python3 "screenshots/poster figures/generate_pillar1_figure.py"
