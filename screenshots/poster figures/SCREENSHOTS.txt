Poster Figures -- SEED symposium poster
=========================================

Figures built specifically for the poster (screenshots/pygame/ holds
the general project screenshots this folder is split out from).
Naming convention: figureN_<description>.png, generated by
generate_figureN_<description>.py in this same folder.


figure1_ftc_field.png -- FTC field + A*-planned path + algorithm comparison
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Built by generate_figure1_ftc_field.py, not captured from an
interactive session like screenshots/pygame's scenarios. Two panels:

  Left  -- an actual FTC-scale (144in x 144in, 6in cells = 24x24) grid
           from ftc.field.build_grid("cluttered"), planned with
           nav.algorithms.astar, robot drawn mid-route with its real
           rotated footprint (the same corner math ftc/field.py's
           collision check uses) over the *eroded* (real, uninflated)
           obstacle cells.
  Right -- Dijkstra vs A* vs RRT vs RRT* planning-time/quality stats,
           recomputed live from benchmark_results/results.csv
           (nav/benchmark.py's 20-trial run) -- the comparison Pillar 1
           cites for why A* is the algorithm every match in this
           project plans with.

Regenerate after any change to ftc/field.py, ftc/match.py, or the
footprint/collision geometry those two share, or after re-running
nav/benchmark.py:

    python3 "screenshots/poster figures/generate_figure1_ftc_field.py"


figure2_reliability_per_dollar.png -- which sensor is the best buy?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Built by generate_figure2_reliability_per_dollar.py. Success-rate gain
over the free DeadReckoningSuite baseline, per $100 spent, at
variance_level >= 0.3 across all 3 deviation types -- recomputed live
from benchmark_results/ftc_suite_results.csv (ftc/suite_benchmark.py's
headline sweep) rather than hardcoded. Bars are sorted best-value-first
and self-labeled with cost + raw success rate, a readability rework of
ftc/suite_benchmark.py's own plot_reliability_per_dollar chart (and an
earlier FTC-Sensor-Study-Poster draft's "Figure 1") -- see the
docstring at the top of the script for the specific problems that
rework fixes.

Regenerate after re-running ftc/suite_benchmark.py (i.e. whenever
benchmark_results/ftc_suite_results.csv changes):

    python3 "screenshots/poster figures/generate_figure2_reliability_per_dollar.py"


figure3_bundle_scatter.png -- every buildable robot: cost vs. success
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Built by generate_figure3_bundle_scatter.py. Cost vs. weighted success
rate (across 5 scenario profiles) for all 23 candidates in
benchmark_results/ftc_optimizer_results.csv (ftc/optimizer.py's bundle
search: individual sensors AND every buildable 2-3 sensor bundle),
deduplicated to 12 visually distinct (cost, success) points -- every
"+IMU" bundle variant lands on the exact same point as its non-IMU
twin, since IMU is free and changes neither cost nor measured success
here, so plotting all 23 raw points would silently overplot 11 of the
12 real positions. All 12 points are labeled (not just the 4-point
Pareto frontier), individual-reachable points are squares and
bundle-only points are circles. A readability rework of
ftc_optimizer_frontier.png's left panel (and an earlier
FTC-Sensor-Study-Poster draft's "Figure 3" left panel).

Regenerate after re-running ftc/optimizer_benchmark.py (i.e. whenever
benchmark_results/ftc_optimizer_results.csv changes):

    python3 "screenshots/poster figures/generate_figure3_bundle_scatter.py"


figure4_scenario_deepdive.png -- which robot wins YOUR scenario?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Built by generate_figure4_scenario_deepdive.py, same source CSV as
figure3. For each of the 5 scenario profiles: the best scenario-specific
robot (individual sensor or bundle, whichever wins, ties broken toward
the cheaper option) vs. figure3's Pareto-best "generalist" bundle
(odometry pods + front camera, $304.99) evaluated on that same
scenario. They tie in 4 of 5 scenarios -- the generalist already is
scenario-optimal there -- except Heavy pose drift, where a cheaper,
scenario-specific bundle measurably beats it. Color coding matches
figure3 (orange = single sensor, blue = bundle, green = the frontier/
generalist pick). A synthesis of an earlier FTC-Sensor-Study-Poster
draft's "Figure 3" right panel and "Figure 4".

Regenerate after re-running ftc/optimizer_benchmark.py (i.e. whenever
benchmark_results/ftc_optimizer_results.csv changes):

    python3 "screenshots/poster figures/generate_figure4_scenario_deepdive.py"
